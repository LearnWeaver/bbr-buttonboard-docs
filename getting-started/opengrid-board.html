The openGrid board#

Every tile case snaps into an openGrid board. Make yours here: set its size in cells and download it, ready to slice.

The board is always the full openGrid profile, 6.8 mm thick and the same on both faces. Cases snap into the front, and the back plate and the low cable tiles snap into the back, which a lite board does not have.

  • No screw holes. The panel isn’t screwed to anything.
  • No edge connectors. One board is one print, so make it the size of your panel, up to what your printer’s bed takes.
  • Cells are on the standard 28 mm pitch, so the board takes any openGrid part, not just the Button Board’s.

Make your board#

The generator needs JavaScript. Without it, a 5 × 5 board is in case-opengrid-board.zip.

Printing#

  • Print it flat on the bed. Either face can be the front, and it needs no supports.
  • A 5 × 5 board is 140 mm square. Big boards take a while: the generator estimates the weight of plastic.
  • Check one cell with a case’s snap before printing a big board. A snap that is too tight or too loose usually means the printer’s dimensions need calibrating, not the board.

Credits#

openGrid is by David D, under CC BY 4.0. This board’s cell profile follows openscad-opengrid (MIT), an implementation of the published openGrid dimensions. It is openGrid compatible, not an official openGrid file.
