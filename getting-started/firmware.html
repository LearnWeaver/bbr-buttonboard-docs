Updating the firmware#

Tiles and the main module update in different ways. Both start from the Firmware screen of the config app.

Tiles#

Tiles update over the bus. Nothing is unplugged and no programmer is needed. One tile updates at a time; the rest of the chain keeps working.

  1. Open Firmware in the config app.
  2. Any tile running older firmware than the images the site carries is listed with an Update button.
  3. Press it and wait. Progress is shown as it goes.

The tile drops out of the chain for a few seconds and rejoins when it is done. Its address and its bindings are untouched.

A tile that loses power partway through an update is not bricked. It comes back on its old firmware.

The main module#

The main module updates over USB by drag-and-drop.

  1. Press Update main module in the config app. The board reboots into its bootloader, and the COM port disappears. This is expected; the config app reports the lost connection.
  2. A USB drive called RPI-RP2 appears on your computer.
  3. Drag the .uf2 file onto it. The drive disappears and the board restarts on the new firmware.

Your configuration — bindings, LED colours, USB personality — survives the update.

If the config app cannot reach the board at all, unplug the board, hold the BOOTSEL button, plug it back in, and release. RPI-RP2 appears the same way.

Which versions am I on?#

The Connect screen reports the main module’s firmware version, and Diagnose reports each tile’s. Mixed versions across tiles are fine. Diagnose flags any tile whose protocol is not compatible.
