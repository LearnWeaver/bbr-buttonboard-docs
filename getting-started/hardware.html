What you have#

The main module#

One per panel. It has the USB-C socket, the processor that runs the bus, and two tile connectors.
Connects to Driver Station laptop, USB-C
Presents itself as A game controller — 32 buttons, 4 axes
Drives Up to 31 tiles on one chain
Also carries The CFG button, and jumpers for bus termination

The main module holds the whole configuration: which key is which button, what colour each LED is, and which USB personality to use. Tiles hold none of it, which is why a tile can be swapped for an identical one without reconfiguring anything.

The tiles#

Every tile is the same on the bus — a small processor, a transceiver, and two connectors — and differs only in what a person touches.

Tile What it is Keys
mx9 3 × 3 grid of Cherry MX hot-swap keys 9
mx9rgb The same, with an RGB LED under every key 9
mx1 A single MX key 1
arc4 Four arcade buttons, each on a 3-pin JST PH lead 4
enc2 Two rotary encoders with push-to-click shafts 2 encoders + 2 keys

Keys on a tile are numbered from 0. Buttons the Driver Station sees are numbered from 1. Those two numbering schemes are separate on purpose — see Binding buttons.

Hot-swap means the key switches push in and pull out by hand, with no soldering. A broken switch in the pit is a ten-second repair.

The terminators#

Two per panel, one at each end of the chain, each on a short cable into the spare connector there. They are not tiles: a small 20 mm board with one connector and one resistor on it, and no electronics. The single connector is deliberate — a terminator cannot be cabled through, so it can only ever sit at an end.

They matter more than they look. A chain with no terminators still works on a short cable and starts dropping tiles on a long one, which is the kind of fault that only shows up at a competition. Fit both and forget about it — see Plugging it together.

What you supply#

  • A USB-C cable to the Driver Station laptop. That is the only power the panel needs.
  • Tile cables — one per link in the chain, six-way JST GH, straight through.
  • Key switches and keycaps for the MX tiles, or arcade buttons for arc4. Any Cherry MX-compatible switch fits.

What the Driver Station sees#

Personality Driver Station You get
frc FRC (Windows) 32 buttons and 4 axes on one HID gamepad
xinput FTC (Driver Hub / phone) An Xbox-360-style gamepad; the 32 buttons map onto its controls

This is a setting, not a different board. Change it in the config app, unplug, plug back in. See The config app.
